What We Change

we target the
whole life,
not the symptom.

The youth development landscape is fragmented, interventions are short, unmeasured, and disconnected from the environments that actually shape young people.

We address this through Youth Precision Work: a structured framework built on five core areas, the SHAPE model.

We don't just run programmes. We establish youth development infrastructure that schools, PRUs, councils and academies can build on.

S
school
How they engage with education and the adults within it.
H
home
The dynamics, stability and relationships in their home environment.
A
aspirations
Their goals, self-belief and vision for their own future.
P
perspectives
How they see themselves, others and the world around them.
E
experiences
The events and encounters that have shaped who they are.
Why Precision

the old model is full.
but is it working?

The young people inside our schools and estates don't need saving, they need the right structure around them. Precision is the difference between activity and outcome.

provision delivers…

One-off sessions that start and stop

Activity counted, impact assumed

Evidence in someone else's filing cabinet

Practitioners working in isolation

precision delivers…

Infrastructure that lasts beyond the funding cycle

Measurable, evidence-based development

A record the young person owns and the commissioner can report

A connected network where every outcome compounds

We don't chase impact. We build the conditions for it.
