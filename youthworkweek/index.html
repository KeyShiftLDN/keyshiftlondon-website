FAQ

the short answers.

is it actually free?+

Yes. Level 1 is free permanently and always has been. For Youth Work Week, Level 2 — normally £34.99 — is also free until 23:59 (GMT) on Sunday 8 November 2026. No card is needed to enrol this week.

what's the difference between level 1 and level 2?+

Level 1 is the foundations — the philosophy, the SHAPE model, the identity tiers and the Precision Formula. Level 2 is application — running real sessions, the Tier Assessment, Precision Aid Cards, safeguarding pathways and measuring outcomes.

how long does each level take?+

Both are self-paced and online. Level 1 is around 6 hours, Level 2 around 8 hours. Start, pause and come back whenever you like — your progress is saved.

do i get a certificate?+

Yes — completing a level earns a free, personalised Precision Graduation Record. To be clear, that is not a professional certification: Certified Precision Practitioner status comes only through Precision Coaching, our PILLAR arc, not the training.

who is precision training for?+

Anyone who works with young people aged 11–18: schools, PRUs, alternative provision, sport academies, councils and community organisations — plus parents, mentors, coaches and pastoral leads.

what happens after 8 november?+

Level 1 stays free, forever. Level 2 simply returns to its normal £34.99. Anything you've already enrolled in stays yours.
