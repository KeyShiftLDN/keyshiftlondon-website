PRE:CORD is the structured youth development ecosystem we built, connecting young people, mentors, schools, clubs and commissioners through one aligned system, built on identity, accountability and measurable growth.

Through the Youth Precision Framework

every interaction has purpose.
every tool has intent.
every outcome is trackable.

young people

commissioners
