The problem

Every practitioner has one.

The one who shuts down. The one who kicks off. The one who's stopped turning up. You've tried the sanctions. You've tried the conversations. You've tried being kind, being firm, being consistent. And still — nothing moves.

Here's what most training never tells you: A young person is never just their behaviour. The defiance, the silence, the bravado, the absence — these are signals, not the story. Behind every one is a context you may not have seen yet. When you can read the context, the behaviour starts to make sense. And once it makes sense, you know what to do.
